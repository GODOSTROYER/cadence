Autoplay tracks When you reach the end of an album, playlist, or selection of songs, Spotify automatically plays similar songs so the music never stops. Pick your device for how to switch Autoplay on and off. Mobile and tablet Tap your profile picture at the top. Tap Settings and privacy Settings . Under Playback , scroll down to Autoplay and switch it on Toggle switched on , or off Toggle switched off . Desktop Click your profile picture at the top, and select Settings . Scroll down to Autoplay and switch it on Toggle switched on , or off Toggle switched off .
