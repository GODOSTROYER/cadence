## Method and scope decisions

Each score, verdict, response kind, flag, severity, and substantive rationale was assigned independently to the exact visible reply. Scripts only assembled the manually specified judgments, copied packet identity fields, calculated integrity hashes, validated the schema, and counted completed records. They did not score or infer judgments.

The evaluation date is 2026-09-18. Frozen current procedures govern; historical replies provide conversational examples only. A supported contact URL does not by itself make a reply useful. Account-security, unresolved entitlement, account-recognition, and compensation requests can appropriately receive honest handoffs. Generic escalation for available app guidance or feature proposals is evaluated against the actual customer request.

Necessary, nonprivate clarification can ship without being called a completed resolution. Public release links and device information are not sensitive account data. Ordinary willingness to inspect supplied public links was not treated as a claim of backstage account access. Explicit staff-forwarding or account-inspection promises were flagged.

Feature-proposal guidance is classified as resolution when it explains how the customer can submit or vote for their idea; transfer to human customer service is classified as handoff. Social acknowledgments remain social even when suitable to ship.
