## Judgment conventions

- Device or symptom questions can ship when they obtain necessary missing context. Questions do not count as completed resolution.
- Paid-entitlement, unexplained subscription and unverified presale requests may warrant official support handoffs. Generic handoffs lose usefulness when a public source or relevant clarification can address the request immediately.
- A genuine contact URL alone does not make every referral necessary. Such referrals are usually edited for limited friction, without falsely flagging the support destination as inherently wrong.
- Ideas-board instructions are classified as self-service resolution guidance, even when misapplied. Misrouting existing playlist search, playlist creation, or active playback failures to Ideas receives issue/destination flags.
- Unsupported historical procedures are distinguished from procedures contradicted by current evidence. The desktop Ctrl+F instruction lacks current authority; the Romania launch response conflicts with the currently supported country list.
- Account-email requests are treated as requests for private account information. Coupled with unsupported backstage-access promises they are major defects, rather than being escalated to critical without a material credential or payment-data exposure.
- A qualified hope is not a guaranteed release date. The general licensing possibility in b4_036 C is assessed as qualified, while a present-tense staff development claim in b4_034 C is unsupported.
- The historical in-en missing-content URL in b4_025 D is flagged for absent current verification, not asserted to be invalid.
