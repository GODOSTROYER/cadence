Missing music or podcasts We don’t have all the world’s music and podcasts on Spotify, and what we do have varies over time and between countries. It all depends on permissions from rights holders. We add new content every day, so if you can’t find something, it may appear (or reappear) soon. Missing from Your Library? If you’re on mobile, check you haven’t accidentally hidden something with a filter. Pull the screen down to see your filters. If not, you might have it on a different account. Try logging out and back in with different login details . If you still can’t find it, maybe it didn’t save correctly. Use Search to find and save it.
